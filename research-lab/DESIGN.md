# A mathematical field notebook

The 3 November 2026 visual direction uses an ink-and-paper palette with limited jade, apricot and violet accents. The purpose is comprehension, not a wall of badges.

## Grammar

Use generous whitespace, an editorial serif heading and readable sans-serif labels. Original SVG assets have a fixed opaque background so they stay legible in GitHub light and dark themes. All graphics must include a title, description, meaningful Markdown alt text and a nearby text equivalent. Never communicate verification status through colour alone.

Use graphs for actual incidence/commutation relationships, rulers for differences, parameter-plane diagrams for complex dynamics and flow charts for dependencies. Label schematic pictures. A finite iteration is not proof of Mandelbrot membership; a fitted box-count slope is not Hausdorff dimension. Keep numerical, exact and conceptual figures visibly separate.

## Assets in this release

- `assets/hero.svg`: original geometric motif, explicitly schematic.
- `assets/research-map.svg`: four research directions. The ruler uses marks {0,1,4,6}; the quadratic drawing uses c(t)=exp(it)/2-exp(2it)/4 and the circle |c+1|=1/4. These familiar component outlines are illustration, not a drawing or proof of the whole Mandelbrot set.
- `assets/research-loop.svg`: the research and correction flow, not an autonomous-program claim.
- `assets/field-witness.svg`: the exact 1129/45 arithmetic witness with its restricted scope.

All four are original editable SVGs under the lab's scoped license. No remote images, scripts, external fonts, analytics, copied paper figures or third-party artwork. They are locally rendered for inspection before publication. The diagram generator is not a theorem prover.

## Future report standard

Start with the question, give one mechanism diagram when it helps, show the proof/checker route, then state the strongest limitation. Do not invent statistics or fill space with decorative charts. Keep text available outside images. For new raster imagery record origin and license; for computed images record the domain, method and finite precision. Test at roughly 900-pixel desktop width and at a smaller width. Avoid embedding tiny captions as the only record of an assumption.
