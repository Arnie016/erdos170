# Open Mathematics Research Lab

Arnav Salkade's open, AI-assisted mathematics notebook: exact finite results, explicit counterexamples, reproducible programs and a growing Lean formalization.

**This is not a claim to have solved Erdős #117 or #170.** A finite case, a computational certificate, an ordinary proof and a Lean-checked theorem are different achievements. Literature priority is separate from mathematical validity.

This lab is added alongside the original sparse-ruler project. The original repository files and experiment history remain intact.

## Research lanes

| Lane | What belongs here | Current boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erdős #117 | Abelian-subgroup covers, noncommuting cliques, binary alternating forms | Low-dimensional finite cases; general problem not claimed solved |
| Modular distinct subset sums | Exact signed-dilation comparisons modulo 2^n+5 | Finite n=6, modulus 69; no all-n theorem |
| Erdős #170 | The original sparse-ruler suite in the repository root | Discrete rulers remain distinct from the continuous autocorrelation parameter tau |
| Lean | Small foundational proofs and explicit finite witnesses | Formalization grows incrementally; a passing build does not formalize every paper in this repository |

## Public record policy

Only the user's original mathematical notes, programs and safe verification artifacts are published. No email threads, contact ledgers, unpublished third-party drafts, credentials or local personal paths belong here. Referenced papers retain their authorship and licensing; they are cited, not redistributed.

Future work should add a dated claim contract, proof or counterexample, independently checked evidence, a dependency/status update and one falsifiable next question. Rejected ideas stay visible as rejected ideas. New contributions should arrive through reviewable commits or pull requests.
