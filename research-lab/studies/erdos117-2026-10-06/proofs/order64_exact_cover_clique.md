# Exact abelian covers for two groups of order 64

Status: matching-witness proof and two executable checks. The explicit binary witness checks are additionally represented in the repository's Lean module. The full group-to-graph reduction is not yet formalized in Lean. No literature novelty is claimed.

For v=(x,y,z,t) in F2^4 and c in F2^2, put (v,c)(w,d)=(v+w,c+d+f(v,w)). Bilinearity of f gives the cocycle identity, hence associativity. The identity is (0,0), and the inverse of (v,c) is (v,c+f(v,v)).

## Case A

Let f(v,w)=(xy',zt'). This is D8 x D8. Its center has order four. The quotient is a direct sum of two symplectic planes.

The three nonzero vectors in each plane form a scalar noncommuting clique. Their 3-by-3 Cartesian product yields a clique of size nine. The nine direct sums of a line in the first plane and a line in the second plane are isotropic and cover every quotient vector. Their full preimages are abelian subgroup covers. As omega<=a for every group, the matching witnesses give

`omega(G_A)=a(G_A)=9`.

## Case B

Let f(v,w)=(xy',yz'). The common radical of the displayed quotient model is <t>, so the actual center has order eight. Modulo that direction, use (x,y,z). The commutator is (xy'+x'y,yz'+y'z).

The four points with y=1 are pairwise noncommuting: their commutator is (x+x',z+z'), nonzero for distinct points. Add (1,0,0), which fails to commute with every one of them. This is a five-clique.

For a matching cover take the whole y=0 plane and the four lines generated by (x,1,z), lifted through the radical and center. They are abelian and cover the group. Therefore

`omega(G_B)=a(G_B)=5`.

These are two specified groups, not a classification of all order-64 groups. A local restriction-rank loss in their earlier use does not imply a global chromatic gap.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 src/worker1_graph_exact.py` and `python3 src/worker2_certificate_check.py` from this directory. Worker 1 checks the full group laws (including 262,144 associativity triples per case), centers and exact graph clique/coloring. Worker 2 separately checks explicit full-group clique and abelian-subgroup cover witnesses by multiplication and rejects corrupted witnesses.
